{# The line under an energy total: "delivered", and "Y kWh in battery" beside it when it differs. `delivered` is the billed figure (`_billed_kwh`: the wallbox counter, else the charger's own kWh where typed, else the battery); the battery figure is the SoC-based one. The pair is what the Ricariche month strip and the search results already say, and the rule for hiding the second number is theirs too: a set of public charges with no meter and nothing typed has ONE number, not two identical ones. Under the number, not in its unit: a summary tile is ~170 px wide on a phone and in the six-column desktop grid alike, and "1296.0 kWh delivered" broke the word onto its own line. `t` is an argument: the translator lives in the render context, not in the globals, and an {% import %} without `with context` does not see it.
