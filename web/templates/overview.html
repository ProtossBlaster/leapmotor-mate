{# The marker follows the car. It was drawn once, at page load, beside a status card that refreshes itself — so the page said "6 s ago" over a position from whenever it was opened. The page embeds main._last_position for its first paint, and the map polls the same builder at /api/last-position, as often as it says. One request at a time, and none while the tab is hidden: a reply can never arrive after a newer one and move the marker back. A request is given one interval, body included, and aborted after it — a reply that late is older than the next one would be, and a connection a proxy left hanging must not stop the map for good. A reply without a usable interval keeps the last one; a reply that says there is no position brings the placeholder back, and anything else that is not a position is treated like a failed poll. The map pans only when the car moves — further than a parked car's GPS wanders — and then recentres on it once it comes within a quarter of the view from an edge, before it reaches the edge. Never under the owner's hand: a move that arrives while the map is being dragged, flung or zoomed waits until it settles; and never because the owner panned away from a car standing still.
