# v3.18.0 — a charge's energy reads the same on every screen

Contributed by [@arekm](https://github.com/arekm) in
[#297](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/pull/297), with a fix of ours on top.

A charge has two energies: what the charger **delivered** — the wallbox counter at home, the
charger's own kWh where you typed them, which is what the cost is computed on — and what reached
the **battery**. The charge card has said both for a long time. Three other places said something
else.

## What changes

- **The Overview's Last charge tile leads with the same figure as the card**, with what reached the
  pack under it: *🔋 12.0 kWh in battery (DC) · efficiency 81 %*. It used to show the battery figure
  alone, beside a cost computed on the other one — a €/kWh that existed on no other screen.

- **Total energy on the Charges page and Energy Charged on Statistics say *delivered*,** with the
  *in battery* figure beside it when the two differ — the pair the month strip already used.

- ⚠️ **Energy Charged on Statistics changes meaning.** It was the battery figure alone; it is now the
  delivered one, with the battery figure beside it. Nothing stored changed: the same charges, read
  under the same rule as every other total.

- **The rule that picks which figure leads is one helper now** (`charge_energy_view`), read by the
  card, by its typed-figure line and by the tile. It used to live inline in two Jinja partials.

## What is fixed

- **Totals over a joined charge were wrong.** They applied the rule to the group's summed columns,
  so a plug-in the car reported in pieces was billed on whatever the meter happened to catch: 12 kWh
  for a 12 + 5 session, and 30 counted where 35 was typed. The sum now runs over the pieces, each on
  its own rule, and a figure you typed counts once for the pieces it covers. The new column
  `charges.gross_kwh_from` records that scope — joining rewrites no row, so nothing else could — and
  its migration reads a legacy figure's scope from the costs that figure left behind.

- **On a joined charge the card leads with what it bills**, under the word the totals use for that
  sum, so the €/kWh beside it divides by the number above it.

- **The charging efficiency was rounded twice, and its own 100 % check read the rounded number**
  (#295). 25.01 kWh into the battery for 25.00 from the wall is 100.04 % — impossible, and exactly
  what the check exists to withhold — but rounded to a tenth it became 100.0 and passed. And 85.48 %
  became 85.5, then 86 on the card, where v3.17.3 read 85. The ratio is returned unrounded now, the
  check reads it unrounded, and each page rounds once for display.

## What does not change

Nothing stored is recomputed. Charges already recorded keep their figures; the numbers that move are
the ones listed above. The database gains one column, which records the scope of a figure you typed.

## Verification

- The full suite passes: **3 583 tests**, 118 of them new.
- Run as an upgrade, on databases **written by v3.17.4 and never migrated before** — the migration's
  backfill only runs when the column is added, so a database migrated by an earlier build keeps its
  old scopes and would have proved nothing.
- Every €/kWh on those databases now lands on a tariff the owner actually set: a joined home charge
  reads 0.25 €/kWh where it read 0.38, and two joined public ones read 0.79 where they read 1.07 and
  1.92. The Charges page's total and its average price divide the same number, which they did not.
- Report, Costs and Wallbox come out identical; so do charges priced by hand, free charges, solar
  charges and charges with no meter at all. No crashes across four servers.
- Checked at 390, 1024 and 1280 px in Italian, Polish and German: the layout comes out as it does on
  v3.17.4.

No command was sent to any vehicle.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- Nothing to do after the update.
- Rolling back to v3.17.4 leaves the new column in place, unused, and the totals read as they did
  before. Nothing stored changes in either direction.

## Italiano

**L'energia di una ricarica si legge uguale su ogni schermata** (#297, @arekm). Una ricarica ha due
energie: quella **erogata** dalla colonnina — il contatore della wallbox a casa, i kWh che scrivi tu
altrove, ed è su quella che si calcola il costo — e quella arrivata **in batteria**. La card le
diceva già tutte e due; tre posti dicevano altro, e adesso parlano come lei: il riquadro **Ultima
ricarica** in Panoramica, **Energia totale** su Ricariche ed **Energia caricata** su Statistiche.
⚠️ **«Energia caricata» cambia significato**: prima era la sola cifra della batteria, ora è quella
erogata con accanto la batteria. Corretti anche i **totali sulle ricariche unite**, che applicavano
la regola alle colonne già sommate del gruppo: una ricarica spezzata in due veniva fatturata su
quello che il contatore aveva preso (12 kWh per una sessione da 12 + 5). Ora la somma scorre sui
pezzi, e una cifra scritta a mano conta una volta sola per i pezzi che copre. Infine il rendimento
della ricarica: veniva arrotondato due volte, e il suo stesso controllo del 100 % leggeva il numero
arrotondato — 100,04 % passava, e 85,48 % diventava 86. Niente viene ricalcolato: le ricariche già
registrate tengono le loro cifre.
