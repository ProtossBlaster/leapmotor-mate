# v3.15.16 — two things from @michapr's own layout

This release continues [beta discussion #31](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/MateBetaTesterOnly/discussions/31).
On 13/09 @michapr attached a working `trip_detail.html` of his own and asked what we thought of it.
Two of its ideas add **no new number** and are simply better, so they are here.

## What changes

- **The trip's cost per distance**, on the line under the total it is derived from: `12.87 €/100 km`
  beneath `12.48 €`. The card already carried the total and the rate the electricity was billed at
  (`0.279 €/kWh`), but not the one figure that lets a 97 km trip be compared with a 12 km one. It is
  written with the same helpers as the Statistics card, so a reader whose distances are in miles
  gets it per 100 mi; on a range extender it spans **both** sources, because the total does.
- **The block of secondary readings at the foot of a trip folds.** Battery level start→end, the
  odometer, average and maximum speed, elevation, outside temperature and the GPS-point count sit
  behind a disclosure instead of eight always-open rows. It ships **open**: folding is offered,
  nothing is taken away from a reader who never touches it.

Nothing else moves. No figure is computed differently, none is dropped, and a car with no tank sees
exactly the same card it saw in v3.15.15 plus the cost per distance.

## The third idea, and why it is not here

His layout also puts **the electricity and the fuel side by side**, as two columns, which is what
[@gm27271's original mockup](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/MateBetaTesterOnly/discussions/31)
asked for. It was built and then measured on the rendered page, and it does not fit this page:

| where | width of each column |
|---|---|
| @michapr's screen (card = full width of a phone) | ~170 px |
| this page at a 1024 px viewport | **87 px** |
| this page at 1600 px | **148 px** |

Mate's trip page is a three-column layout and the summary card is the **first column**, not the
whole width. Halving it gives 87–148 px, and the fuel area then splits its own width again into two
cells — about 70 px for `5.4 L/100km`, which is the mid-figure wrapping this card was rebuilt to
remove. The two areas stay stacked, each using the full width of the column.

## Verification

Ten tests were added and each was seen to fail first — nine by rendering the previous template, the
tenth by removing the division's guard on a copy, which takes the page down with a division by zero
rather than merely dropping a line. Two of them hold the **decision** above, so the side-by-side is
not re-taken without measuring the column again. Full suite: 3326 passing.

The card was rendered at 1440 px and at 375 px and read at both, for a range-extender trip that
burned petrol and for a car with no tank; the fold was opened and closed. No live vehicle-cloud
command was sent.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- MateDesktop receives the payload through its existing updater; no new shell installer is needed.
- This release changes one template and the eight translation files: no database schema, no stored
  data, no MQTT entity, and no migration.
- Rolling back to v3.15.15 requires no data conversion.

## Italiano

Sulla scheda di un viaggio compare **il costo per distanza**, sotto il totale da cui nasce:
`12,87 €/100 km` sotto `12,48 €`. La scheda aveva il totale e il prezzo a cui era stata contata
l'elettricità, ma non la cifra che permette di confrontare un viaggio di 97 km con uno di 12. È
scritta con lo stesso denominatore della pagina Statistiche, quindi chi legge in miglia la vede per
100 mi; su una REEV comprende **tutte e due** le fonti, perché il totale le comprende.

E **il blocco delle letture secondarie in fondo al viaggio ora si richiude**: livello della batteria
inizio→fine, contachilometri, velocità media e massima, dislivello, temperatura esterna e numero di
punti GPS stanno dietro una linguetta invece di otto righe sempre aperte. Arriva **aperto**: la
piegatura è offerta, niente viene tolto a chi non la tocca.

Tutte e due vengono dal layout che @michapr ha costruito e allegato alla discussione beta #31. La
sua terza idea — elettricità e carburante **affiancati** invece che impilati — è stata costruita,
misurata sulla pagina vera e tolta: è giusta sul suo schermo, dove la scheda è larga quanto un
telefono, ed è sbagliata sulla nostra, dove la scheda è la prima colonna di una pagina a tre
colonne. Misurate lì, le due colonne vengono **87 px** con una finestra da 1024 e **148 px** a 1600,
e l'area del carburante si divide di nuovo a metà: circa 70 px per `5,4 L/100km`, cioè l'a capo in
mezzo a una cifra che questa scheda è stata rifatta per togliere.

Nessun numero è calcolato in modo diverso e nessuno è stato tolto. Cambia un template e le otto
traduzioni: nessuna modifica al database, ai dati salvati o alle entità MQTT, e il ritorno alla
v3.15.15 non richiede conversioni.
