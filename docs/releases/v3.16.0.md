# v3.16.0 — the price you type keeps the charge's type

The work of [@hubcasale](https://github.com/hubcasale) in
[PR #284](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/pull/284), reviewed over six rounds —
and a charge that could be booked at nineteen hours instead of four, reported by
[@juan-conca](https://github.com/juan-conca) in
[#289](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/289).

## What changes

- **The total you actually paid is typed in a ✎ of its own.** On a public charger with a
  complicated tariff — a subscription, a session fee — the figure on the receipt is the only true
  one. Until now it was typed by picking **Manual** on the charge's type badge, and that choice took
  the place of Home, AC, Fast or HPC in the same field: the charge could no longer be found by type,
  and its badge read *✎ Manual* instead of what it had been. The total now goes into a **✎** beside
  the badge and is stored in a column of its own, so the badge only ever answers *what kind of charge
  was this*. Confirming or correcting a type no longer recomputes a price that was typed by hand.

- **The cost on the card says where its figure comes from.** A small **est.** means Mate computed it
  from the price set for that charge type; **billed** means it is the total you typed. *Reset*, in
  the ✎, goes back to the computed one. Before, that distinction lived in a tooltip that only two
  actions ever set, so on a normally loaded page it was not there at all.

- **A charge left on the old Manual type reads ❓ To confirm** and keeps the price typed on it. One
  click on its badge puts back the type it really had, and that click does not touch the price. Mate
  does not guess the type itself: what a *Manual* charge was before cannot be read off the row, and a
  wrong guess would quietly reprice real money — an HPC session at the DC rate, a home session as
  public AC.

- **A Home vs Public card on the Charges page**, beside *AC vs DC*: **Home**, **Public** and **To
  confirm**, as a donut and three tiles. The three always add up to the number of charges written
  above them, so a charge still waiting for its type is shown as waiting instead of being counted as
  public.

- **A charge no longer stays open because the car fell asleep.** When the car stops talking the
  Leapmotor cloud does not say so: it hands back the last frame it holds, over and over. If the
  current had just stopped with the cable still in — a charge that finished, or a programmed window
  that ended — that repeated frame keeps reading *cable connected*, which is exactly what holds a
  session open across a wallbox pause. So the charge stayed open until the car next woke up: on the
  install that reported this, four hours of charging were booked as **nineteen**, and the row was
  not in the list at all until it closed, because the list only shows charges that have ended. Mate
  now gives up on a charge that is being held open by nothing but a frame the cloud has repeated for
  thirty minutes, and dates it from the last reading taken while current was actually flowing.

  Two cases are deliberately left alone. A wallbox that modulates pauses while the car is awake and
  pushing **new** frames, so the session stays whole exactly as before — it is frame identity that
  separates the two, not a timer on the data's age. And a frozen frame that still reports current is
  a car charging somewhere its link cannot reach, whose charge reappears when it does; that belongs
  to the reconstruction, and is untouched.

## What does not change

Nothing already stored is repriced or retyped by the upgrade. Charge types, costs, energy figures,
the AC vs DC card, the Statistics and Costs pages, the MQTT entities and the export all keep what
they had. The database gains one column, `cost_manual`, marked for the charges whose price had been
typed by hand — that is the whole migration.

## Verification

The change was reviewed in six rounds on the pull request; each round was tested here before the
next one was asked for. On the version merged:

- the full suite passes — 3369 tests — and CI is green on the merge commit;
- the tests that guard the new counting were each seen failing: the test file of the last round run
  against the previous version, and then, on a copy of the merged code with the old counting put back
  by hand, the new test and the one asserting that Home + Public + To confirm equals the total;
- an install of v3.15.18 with six charges — one never confirmed, two on the old *Manual* type, an HPC
  priced by hand, and a DC session — was upgraded and used through the page in a browser: the ✎ typed
  on a charge that was never confirmed and on an old *Manual* one, and a type picked from the badge on
  a DC session. The three tiles agreed with the donut and with the page header at every step;
- rolling back to v3.15.18 on that same install was tried too (see below).

For the charge fix, the reporter's own diagnostics bundle — 38 412 polls over twelve days — was
replayed frame by frame through the recorder. On the code as it stood, the replay reproduces the row
in his database exactly: 1 138.5 minutes, ending at 06:59 the next morning. On the fixed code the
same readings produce 239.3 minutes ending at 15:59:54, with the same 7.465 kWh, the same start and
end SoC and the same peak power. The thirty-minute limit was measured rather than chosen: in that
bundle, while current is genuinely flowing the frame is 2 s old at the median, 44 s at the 99th
percentile and 832 s at its worst, and not one of the 9 224 frozen readings ever claimed current.
Eleven tests cover it, each one watched failing first — against the previous code, and against four
mutations of the new one.

No command was sent to any vehicle: the tests and the trial install use test data.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- MateDesktop receives the payload through its existing updater; no new shell installer is needed.
- The database gains the `cost_manual` column on the first start; existing rows priced by hand are
  marked in it. No data is converted or moved.
- Charges already recorded keep the duration they were given, including any that were left open
  overnight: Mate does not rewrite history it cannot re-measure. The change applies to charges
  recorded from this version on.
- Rolling back to v3.15.18 leaves every charge as it is: the older version ignores the new column,
  and the prices and types already stored stay. What comes back with it is the old behaviour — the ✎
  beside the badge is gone, *Manual* is a type in the menu again, and confirming the type of a charge
  whose price you had typed recomputes that price from the rate for the type (in the trial install a
  charge typed at 22,00 € came back as 23,60 € once its type was confirmed again on v3.15.18).

## Italiano

**Il prezzo che scrivi a mano non costa più il tipo alla ricarica** (PR #284, @hubcasale). Fino alla
v3.15.18 il totale realmente pagato si scriveva scegliendo il tipo *Manuale*, che prendeva il posto di
Casa, AC, Veloce o HPC nello stesso campo: la ricarica spariva dalle ricerche per tipo e il suo
distintivo diceva *✎ Manuale* invece di quello che era. Adesso il totale si scrive nella **✎** accanto
al distintivo e sta in una colonna sua, e il distintivo risponde solo a *che tipo di ricarica era*.
Confermare o correggere il tipo non ricalcola più un prezzo scritto a mano, e il costo sulla scheda
dice da dove viene: **stima** se l'ha calcolato Mate, **fatturato** se l'hai scritto tu (*Ripristina*
rimette la stima).

Una ricarica rimasta sul vecchio tipo *Manuale* si legge **❓ Da confermare** e tiene il prezzo che ci
avevi scritto; un clic sul distintivo le rimette il tipo vero senza toccare quel prezzo. Mate non lo
indovina da solo: dalla riga non si può sapere cosa fosse, e sbagliare vorrebbe dire riprezzare soldi
veri.

Nella pagina Ricariche, accanto alla card *AC vs DC*, ce n'è una nuova: **Casa vs Pubblica**, con
Casa, Pubblica e Da confermare. Le tre caselle fanno sempre il numero di ricariche scritto sopra, così
una ricarica che aspetta il suo tipo si vede, invece di finire contata fra le pubbliche.

**E una ricarica non resta più aperta perché l'auto si è addormentata** (#289, @juan-conca). Quando
l'auto smette di parlare il cloud non lo dice: ripete l'ultimo fotogramma che ha. Se la corrente era
appena finita col cavo ancora attaccato, quel fotogramma continua a dire *cavo collegato* — che è
proprio ciò che tiene insieme la sessione quando la wallbox fa una pausa — e la ricarica restava
aperta fino al risveglio dell'auto: quattro ore di ricarica contate **diciannove**, e nell'elenco non
si vedeva niente finché non si chiudeva. Adesso Mate rinuncia a una ricarica tenuta in piedi solo da
un fotogramma che il cloud ripete da mezz'ora, e la data all'ultima lettura presa mentre passava
corrente davvero. Restano intoccate la pausa della wallbox (arriva su fotogrammi **nuovi**, l'auto è
sveglia) e l'auto che sta caricando dove il collegamento non arriva (il suo fotogramma dice ancora
corrente). Energia, percentuali e picco erano già giusti e non cambiano.

Niente di già salvato viene riprezzato o ritipizzato dall'aggiornamento: il database prende solo la
colonna `cost_manual`. Tornando alla v3.15.18 le ricariche restano come sono, ma torna anche il
comportamento di prima: la ✎ sparisce, *Manuale* torna un tipo nel menu, e riconfermare il tipo di una
ricarica col prezzo scritto a mano lo ricalcola (nell'installazione di prova una ricarica scritta a
22,00 € è tornata 23,60 €). Le ricariche già registrate tengono la durata che hanno, comprese quelle
rimaste aperte una notte: Mate non riscrive una storia che non può rimisurare.
