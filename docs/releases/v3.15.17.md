# v3.15.17 — a T03 west of Greenwich stays west, and a certificate Mate cannot read is refused

Two fixes, both from diagnostics bundles attached to the issues that reported them.

## What changes

- **A T03 west of the Greenwich meridian is drawn where it is** ([#282](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/282)).
  The report: a home about 30 km west of the line, work 100 m east of it, and the drive to work drawn
  as 30 km heading **east**. The bundle showed the longitude arriving from the car with its minus,
  and the trip starts stored without it.

  Every car that had this symptom before was a C10 or a B10. Those send each coordinate twice: once
  signed, once as a bare magnitude, and Mate uses the signed one. The T03 sends it **once, already
  signed**, as a named field — and Mate filed it only in the magnitude slot. The signed slot was
  mapped to a field name that no T03 response contains, so it was always empty: the minus was
  dropped, and the hemisphere Mate put back could only have been learned from positions already
  stored without one. Now the T03's `latitude` and `longitude` fill the signed slots, in both places
  that read them (the poller, and the Refresh button).

  Nothing changes for a car east of Greenwich. A C10 or B10 does not go through this code.

- **A certificate Mate cannot read is refused when it is uploaded** ([#283](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/283)).
  The report was "no login", and the bundle showed every login failing with
  `SSLError(524297, '[SSL] PEM lib (_ssl.c:3855)')` — which, reproduced in the add-on image, is
  OpenSSL saying the **certificate** file cannot be read. The wizard had accepted it because it only
  looked for the text `-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----`, and a file cut short, a certificate flattened
  onto one line or the saved web page that displays it all contain that line.

  The two files are now opened the way the login opens them, before they are kept. A pair that does
  not open is refused, and the wizard says in its own language which part is wrong: the certificate,
  the key, or a key that belongs to a different certificate. The pair already saved is not touched by
  a refused upload. And a saved certificate that cannot be read no longer counts as present, so the
  wizard shows the certificate step again instead of skipping to a login that cannot succeed.

## What does not change

Positions recorded before this release keep the side they were stored on. Mate stores the
coordinate it has already interpreted, not the raw signal, and near the meridian there is no telling
which stored rows were really west — so none are rewritten.

A file saved on Windows (CRLF line endings, a byte-order mark) opens normally and is accepted.

## Verification

**#282** — 7 tests: five failed on v3.15.16 with the exact symptom (`+1.2577` for a car at
`-1.2577`), the other two (a T03 in Milan does not move; the diagnostics bundle still carries no
coordinate through the newly filled slots) were seen to fail on a mutated copy. A simulation ran the
real poller code against a database whose history was already stored without the minus, with a
restart every night as in the reporter's log: on v3.15.16, **2 of 82** points of the home–work–home
drive were on the right side, every day for six days; with this release, **82 of 82** from the first
day. A T03 in Milan is identical before and after.

**#283** — 11 tests, plus one that runs the wizard's own `saveCert()` in node for all eight
languages. Nine failed on v3.15.16; the others were seen to fail on mutated copies. The wizard was
also driven in a browser against a scratch instance: a certificate on one line was refused in
Italian with nothing written, the correct pair went through to the sign-in step, and a broken
certificate placed on disk brought the certificate step back.

Full suite: 3344 passing. No live vehicle-cloud command was sent.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- MateDesktop receives the payload through its existing updater; no new shell installer is needed
  (the check uses `ssl`, which the shell already carries).
- No database schema, stored data, MQTT entity or migration changes.
- Rolling back to v3.15.16 requires no data conversion; a T03 west of Greenwich would be mirrored
  again from that point on.

## Italiano

**Una T03 a ovest del meridiano di Greenwich viene disegnata dove si trova** (#282). La segnalazione:
casa circa 30 km a ovest della linea, lavoro 100 m a est, e il tragitto disegnato come 30 km verso
**est**. Tutte le auto che avevano avuto questo problema prima erano C10 o B10, che mandano ogni
coordinata due volte — una col segno e una senza — e Mate usa quella col segno. La T03 la manda
**una volta sola, già col segno**, e Mate la metteva solo nella casella dei numeri senza segno; la
casella col segno leggeva un nome di campo che nessuna risposta della T03 contiene. Così il meno si
perdeva sempre. Ora `latitude` e `longitude` della T03 vanno nelle caselle col segno, sia nel poller
sia nel pulsante Aggiorna. A est di Greenwich non cambia niente, e C10 e B10 non passano da qui. Le
posizioni già salvate restano come sono: vicino al meridiano non si può sapere quali fossero davvero
a ovest.

**Un certificato che Mate non riesce a leggere viene rifiutato al caricamento** (#283). La procedura
guidata controllava solo che dentro ci fosse la scritta `-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----`, e un file
tagliato, un certificato su una riga sola o la pagina web salvata al posto del file la contengono
tutti: il file veniva salvato e ogni login falliva con `[SSL] PEM lib`. Ora i due file vengono aperti
come li apre il login prima di tenerli; se non si aprono vengono rifiutati, e il messaggio — nella
lingua della procedura — dice cosa non va: il certificato, la chiave, o una chiave che appartiene a un
altro certificato. La coppia già salvata non viene toccata. E un certificato salvato prima che non si
legge non conta più come presente: la procedura ripropone il passo del certificato invece di saltare a
un login che non può funzionare.

Nessuna modifica al database, ai dati salvati o alle entità MQTT; il ritorno alla v3.15.16 non
richiede conversioni.
