# v3.17.0 — the two commands Home Assistant never got, and a word on every charge

Reported by [@Kuli1111](https://github.com/Kuli1111) in
[#292](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/292), who noticed that the A/C could
be switched off from Home Assistant but not simply switched on.

## What changes

- **A/C Auto reaches Home Assistant.** The Commands page has always had the plain *turn the climate
  on*: the car decides by itself whether to cool or heat, and works toward the target temperature
  without engaging cool, heat or vent. The MQTT bridge never carried that one. It advertised Quick
  Cool, Quick Heat, Quick Ventilation, Defrost and A/C Off — five of the page's six tiles — so an
  automation could start the climate only by pinning a mode, and the button that stops it had no
  counterpart that starts it. **A/C Auto** now sends exactly what the page sends, with the target
  temperature read from that car's own last reading. On a T03 it falls back to manual cooling: that
  firmware ignores an automatic write, and cooling is the one mode it is known to honour.

- **Preheat Battery reaches Home Assistant.** The same kind of gap, found while answering the first
  one and reported by nobody: the Quick action that warms the battery before a fast charge existed
  on the page and not over MQTT. It is a button like the others now.

- **Every charge records why it stopped.** Six reasons, one word each: the cable was unplugged, the
  charge was deferred to its programmed window, the car went quiet, it was driven away, the cloud
  stopped answering, or the charge was reconstructed after a blackout. The word is written at the
  moment the charge closes and shown in the diagnostics bundle beside `recon=`. It is there for
  reports like [#289](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/289), where the
  question that took a day to answer — *what actually ended this charge* — was not written down
  anywhere.

## What does not change

Nothing on the pages moves. No entity is renamed: both buttons carry keys that have never been
published before, so the entity ids Home Assistant has already registered are untouched, and an
existing automation keeps working exactly as written. The charge pages, the statistics, the costs
and the export are as they were. The database gains one column, `close_reason`, empty for every
charge already recorded — the reason is written from this version on, and no past charge is
re-interpreted.

## Verification

- The full suite passes: **3 396 tests**, and CI is green on both commits of this release
  (`d104c59`, `823881c`).
- The eight tests covering the two new commands were each **watched failing first**, before the code
  existed: six for A/C Auto — the button, the body sent, the target temperature carried through, the
  clamp, the T03 fallback and the other models left in automatic — and two for the battery preheat.
- The body A/C Auto sends was taken from the page's own command rather than written afresh, and the
  T03 fallback is the one already applied on that side.
- The gap itself was measured, not guessed: the commands the page has were listed against the
  commands the bridge accepts, and `ac_on` and `battery_preheat` were the two present on one side
  only. The bridge ends its dispatch with a plain refusal, so before this release no payload could
  reach either — the buttons were missing and so was any way around them.
- The charge reason ships with eight tests of its own, one of which asserts that every charge Mate
  writes carries a reason, and CI was green on it when it landed.

No command was sent to any vehicle: every test drives a fake cloud client.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- The database gains the `close_reason` column on the first start. Nothing is converted or moved.
- The two new buttons appear in Home Assistant within a few seconds of the update, with no action
  needed beyond having MQTT enabled as before.
- Rolling back to v3.16.0 removes the two buttons from Home Assistant again and stops the reason
  being written; the column and the words already stored are ignored by the older version and are
  still there if you come forward again. An automation that had started using **A/C Auto** or
  **Preheat Battery** stops working on the older version — those are the only two things a rollback
  takes away.

## Italiano

**A/C Auto e Preriscaldamento batteria arrivano in Home Assistant** (#292, @Kuli1111). Nella pagina
Comandi ci sono sei riquadri del clima; il ponte MQTT ne pubblicava cinque. Quello che mancava era
proprio l'accensione semplice — *accendi il clima*, e l'auto decide da sola se raffreddare o
scaldare puntando alla temperatura impostata: da un'automazione si poteva far partire il clima solo
scegliendo Quick Cool, Quick Heat o Ventilazione, che fissano un modo, e il pulsante che spegne non
aveva quello che accende. Adesso **A/C Auto** manda esattamente ciò che manda la pagina, temperatura
di destinazione compresa, presa dall'ultima lettura di quell'auto; sulla T03 ripiega sul
raffreddamento manuale, l'unico modo che quel firmware rispetta. Rispondendo a quella segnalazione è
saltato fuori lo stesso buco su **Preriscaldamento batteria**, che nessuno aveva mai segnalato: c'era
nella pagina e non su MQTT. Nessuna entità è stata rinominata — le due chiavi sono nuove, quindi
niente di già installato cambia nome.

**E ogni ricarica registra perché si è fermata**: cavo staccato, carica rimandata alla finestra
programmata, auto zitta, partita, cloud che non risponde, oppure ricostruita dopo un buco. La parola
si scrive quando la ricarica si chiude e si legge nel pacchetto diagnostico accanto a `recon=`. Serve
per le segnalazioni come la #289, dove la domanda che è costata una giornata — *cosa ha chiuso questa
ricarica* — non era scritta da nessuna parte. Le ricariche già registrate restano senza motivo: si
scrive da questa versione in poi.

Tornando alla v3.16.0 i due pulsanti spariscono da Home Assistant e il motivo smette di essere
scritto; la colonna e le parole già salvate restano lì, ignorate. Se un'automazione aveva cominciato
a usare A/C Auto o il preriscaldamento, con la versione vecchia smette di funzionare: è l'unica cosa
che il ritorno indietro porta via.
