# v3.18.2 — session recovery stops hammering a cloud that is refusing

Reported by [@gm27271](https://github.com/gm27271) (beta #49,
[#295](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/295)) and
[@adoewa](https://github.com/adoewa) ([#296](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/296)).

Three installs whose logs reach 17–18/09 break the same way, line for line:

```
04:34:41 [ERROR]   Poll error: ... Read timed out
04:34:41 [INFO]    Attempting session recovery (re-login)…
04:34:42 [WARNING] Re-login failed, will retry next cycle: Leapmotor login failed: Error occurred
```

The cloud drops a poll, Mate asks for a new token — the right move — and the cloud refuses it.

## What is fixed

- **The gap between attempts grows while the refusals continue.** 60 s, then 120, 240, 480, 960,
  and no longer than half an hour. One accepted login puts it straight back to 60 s. Four days of
  continuous refusal now cost about **196 attempts instead of 5 271**, and recovery still lands
  within half an hour of the cloud relenting.
- **The first retry is unchanged at 60 seconds.** This is also the path that heals a vanished
  `/tmp` certificate or a single dropped token — the cases it was written for, fixed by the first
  or second attempt. Those installs behave exactly as they did before.
- **The warning says when the next attempt is** instead of "will retry next cycle", which stopped
  being true.

## What this is not

This does not bring a refused car back online. The refusal comes from the cloud and stays the
cloud's: it is not a blocked account — logins keep being accepted now and then right through the
outage, one of them ninety seconds after the last refusal on one install. What the account loses is
the rate. The cloud accepted ~310 logins a day from one install until 16/09 and 5–12 a day from the
17th; the three days before that carry **zero** refusals across thousands of polls, and four other
bundles whose window ends before 17/09 are clean. No Mate version, and no change in
`leapmotor-api` (pinned at 0.3.1 since 05/06), touches the login path.

What changed is our side of it: a fixed one-minute retry asked 1 440 times a day — 5 271 failed
attempts in four days for ~79 accepted on one install, 4 204 for 14 on another — which hammers the
very thing that is rate-limiting us and buries the real cause under a wall of warnings.

## Documentation

- **The Charge Schedule JSON is documented in full**, English and Italian: the five accepted keys
  and their types, `days` as a Monday-first mask (the official app *displays* the week
  Sunday-first), what merging does to the keys you omit, the single exception for `soc`, and what a
  refused command leaves untouched. Every line was checked by running the handler. Raised by
  [@jcconca](https://github.com/jcconca) in PR #304, from discussion #302.
- **The plan field behind the app's "keep charging past the window" checkbox is named.** Mate has
  always read it and written it back unchanged, and it still cannot be set through the JSON; until
  now the documentation could not say which field it was.
  [@juan-conca](https://github.com/juan-conca) identified it on a real car in discussion #306.
- **The README version banner is current again** — it had stood at v3.15.9 for eight releases.
  Spotted by [@jcconca](https://github.com/jcconca) in PR #305.

## Verification

- The full suite passes: **3 599 tests**, seven of them new and each watched fail first.
- Seven mutations of the new code on a copy, all caught: the offset that keeps the first retry at
  60 s, the cap, the doubling itself, the reset on success, the increment on failure, the gate
  reading the new wait rather than the old hard-coded minute, and the half-hour ceiling.
- Every row of the new Charge Schedule table was produced by running `_apply_charge_schedule`
  against a plan, not by reading the code: the five keys, an ignored unknown key, the merge, the
  `soc` fallback and its refusal, both out-of-range refusals, two malformed times, the round-trip
  of the two untouched fields, and the five-key echo.
- Both README sections were rendered through GitHub's own Markdown renderer: one table, six rows,
  fifteen cells, nested inside its list item, identical in English and Italian.

No command was sent to any vehicle.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- Nothing to do after the update.
- Rolling back to v3.18.1 restores the fixed one-minute retry and changes nothing else; nothing
  stored differs in either direction.

## Italiano

**Il recupero della sessione smette di martellare un cloud che sta rifiutando** (beta #49 @gm27271,
#295, #296 @adoewa). Su tre installazioni i cui log arrivano al 17–18/09 il guasto è identico, riga
per riga: il cloud lascia cadere un poll, Mate chiede un token nuovo — la cosa giusta — e il cloud
lo rifiuta. **Non è un account bloccato**: i login continuano a passare ogni tanto per tutta la
durata del disservizio, uno a novanta secondi dall'ultimo rifiuto. Quello che l'account perde è il
ritmo: fino al 16/09 il cloud accettava ~310 login al giorno da un'installazione, dal 17 ne accetta
5–12. Nei tre giorni prima i rifiuti sono **zero** su migliaia di poll, e altri quattro bundle la
cui finestra finisce prima del 17/09 sono puliti. Nessuna versione di Mate e nessun cambio in
`leapmotor-api` (ferma alla 0.3.1 dal 05/06) tocca la strada del login.

Quello che era nostro è il ritentativo: un minuto fisso vuol dire 1.440 tentativi al giorno — 5.271
falliti in quattro giorni per ~79 accettati su un'installazione, 4.204 per 14 su un'altra. Ora il
divario **raddoppia a ogni rifiuto consecutivo** e si ferma a mezz'ora (60s, 120, 240, 480, 960,
1800): quattro giorni costano ~196 tentativi invece di 5.271, e il rientro arriva comunque entro
mezz'ora da quando il cloud smette. Un login riuscito lo riporta subito a 60 s. **Il primo
ritentativo resta a 60 secondi**, perché questa stessa strada ripara anche il certificato sparito in
`/tmp` o un token caduto una volta sola, casi che si risolvono al primo tentativo: quelle
installazioni non rallentano di un secondo. Questo **non** riporta online un'auto rifiutata — il
rifiuto è del cloud e resta suo; smettiamo di peggiorarlo e di seppellire la causa vera.

**Documentazione.** Il JSON della programmazione ricarica è documentato per intero, in inglese e in
italiano: le cinque chiavi accettate, `days` come maschera con il lunedì per primo (l'app ufficiale
*mostra* la settimana con la domenica per prima), cosa fa la fusione con le chiavi che ometti,
l'unica eccezione del `soc`, e cosa lascia intatto un comando rifiutato — ogni riga verificata
eseguendo il gestore, non leggendolo (PR #304 di @jcconca, dalla discussione #302). È nominato il
campo dietro la casella «continua a caricare oltre la finestra» dell'app: Mate l'ha sempre letto e
riscritto invariato e continua a non essere impostabile dal JSON, ma fino a ieri non potevamo dire
quale fosse — l'ha individuato @juan-conca su un'auto vera (discussione #306). E il banner di
versione del README è di nuovo aggiornato: era fermo alla v3.15.9 da otto rilasci (PR #305).
