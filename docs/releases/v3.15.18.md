# v3.15.18 — the notes of a joined trip show local time

One fix, reported with two screenshots on the issue that introduced the notes.

## What changes

- **The later notes of a joined trip show their times in local time** ([#279](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/279)).
  Since v3.15.10 a joined trip keeps the note of every piece, and each later note is headed by that
  piece's start and end time. Those times were printed exactly as Mate stores them, in UTC, while the
  trip's own time at the top of the page was converted to local time. The report: a trip at **05:53**,
  and below it notes at **04:04 → 04:28** and **04:30 → 04:37** — two hours early, the summer offset
  in Italy.

  The later notes now go through the same conversion as the trip's time, in the zone set in Mate's
  settings, with the offset in force on that date.

## What does not change

Nothing stored is touched: the times were right in the database and wrong only on the page. The
first note of the trip, the stops drawn on its chart, and every other page that shows a time were
already converted and are unchanged.

## Verification

A new test file holds five cases: the reporter's morning in Europe/Rome, the same morning in winter
(one hour, not two), a zone west of Greenwich (America/New_York), a zone that is UTC, and the real
trip page rendered through the web app. Four failed on v3.15.17 with the reported times; the UTC case
passes on both, as it should. The v3.15.10 test that had asserted the stored UTC strings was updated
and fails without the fix.

The page was opened in a browser on a scratch instance with the reporter's three pieces joined:
v3.15.17 showed 05:53 above 04:04 → 04:28 and 04:30 → 04:37; this release shows 05:53 above
06:04 → 06:28 and 06:30 → 06:37. A simulation over 2026 joined a trip every seven hours (1,252
trips) and put pieces across both daylight-saving changes: no printed time differed from the local
clock.

Full suite: 3349 passing. No live vehicle-cloud command was sent.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- MateDesktop receives the payload through its existing updater; no new shell installer is needed.
- No database schema, stored data, MQTT entity or migration changes.
- Rolling back to v3.15.17 requires no data conversion; the later notes of a joined trip would show
  UTC again.

## Italiano

**Le note di un viaggio unito mostrano l'ora locale** (#279). Dalla v3.15.10 un viaggio unito tiene la
nota di ogni pezzo, e sopra ogni nota successiva c'è l'ora di inizio e di fine di quel pezzo. Quelle
ore venivano stampate come Mate le salva, in UTC, mentre l'ora del viaggio in cima alla pagina era già
convertita in ora locale. La segnalazione: un viaggio alle **05:53** e, sotto, note alle **04:04 →
04:28** e **04:30 → 04:37**, due ore prima. Ora le note passano dalla stessa conversione dell'ora del
viaggio, nel fuso impostato in Mate e con lo scarto in vigore in quella data.

Nessun dato salvato cambia: le ore erano giuste nel database e sbagliate solo sulla pagina. Il ritorno
alla v3.15.17 non richiede conversioni.
