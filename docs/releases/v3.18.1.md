# v3.18.1 — the all-trips average divides its own kilometres

Reported by [@arzthilfe](https://github.com/arzthilfe) in
[#303](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/303).

One screen, two figures for the same driving: the tile said **20.3 kWh/100 km**, the *Consumption ·
all trips* card said **56.1**, and beside the card stood 156 km and 30.3 kWh — which is 19.4. His
arithmetic was right; the card's was not.

## What is fixed

- **The average divides the energy of the trips whose kilometres it counts.** The card used to
  divide the energy the cloud reports **for the whole period** by the kilometres of the trips that
  carry a cloud figure *of their own*. The cloud answers on dates and knows nothing about Mate's
  trips, so those two describe the same driving only while every trip carries a figure. A trip keeps
  its kilometres while carrying none whenever it started before that feature was switched on, did
  not settle within its six-hour re-fetch window, was never reached by the background sweep, or had
  its reading refused as implausible. The pair is now the energy and the kilometres of one and the
  same set of trips — what the month and day strips have always divided.

- **The error grew as coverage thinned, with nothing to stop it.** 30.3 kWh over 54 of 156 km gave
  56.1; measured on the same code, a hundred trips with two covered gave 900.0 kWh/100 km, printed
  as plainly as any other figure.

- **Three places drew that card** and all three are corrected: the **Trips** page, the **Monthly
  report** and the period views (day / week / month / all trips).

## What does not change

- The line underneath still names the kilometres the average speaks for — *"over 54 km"*.
- Distance, Duration and the driving / climate / other split are the same figures as before.
- Where every trip carries a cloud figure — nearly always — the average reads exactly as it did.
- The per-month and per-day strips were already right and are untouched.
- Nothing stored is recomputed: no trip, charge or cost changes.

## Verification

- The full suite passes: **3 592 tests**, nine of them new and each watched fail first.
- Six mutations of the new code on a copy, all caught by those tests. A seventh survived and was
  removed instead of covered: it was a second guard saying what the first already said.
- Measured in the page, not only in the code: v3.18.0 and this build served side by side on the same
  database and the same cloud total — **56.1 before, 18.0 after**, on the Trips card, the Monthly
  report and the period view, with the line underneath unchanged.

No command was sent to any vehicle.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- Nothing to do after the update.
- Rolling back to v3.18.0 brings the old average back and changes nothing else; nothing stored
  differs in either direction.

## Italiano

**La media di tutti i viaggi divide i chilometri che conta davvero** (#303, @arzthilfe). Su una
schermata sola comparivano due cifre per la stessa guida: il riquadro diceva **20,3 kWh/100 km**, la
card *Consumo · tutti i viaggi* diceva **56,1**, e accanto stavano 156 km e 30,3 kWh — che fanno
19,4. La card divideva l'energia che il cloud riporta **per tutto il periodo** per i soli chilometri
dei viaggi che hanno una cifra dal cloud **loro**: il cloud risponde su date e non conosce i viaggi
di Mate, quindi le due cose descrivono la stessa guida solo finché ogni viaggio porta la sua cifra.
Un viaggio tiene i suoi chilometri e resta senza cifra quando è partito prima che quella funzione
fosse accesa, non si è stabilizzato entro le sue sei ore, non è stato raggiunto dal recupero in
sottofondo, o la lettura è stata rifiutata perché implausibile. Ora la coppia è l'energia e i
chilometri degli **stessi** viaggi — la formula che le strisce del mese e del giorno usano da
sempre. L'errore cresceva al calare della copertura senza alcun freno: cento viaggi con due coperti
stampavano **900,0 kWh/100 km**. Corrette le tre schermate che disegnano quella card: **Viaggi**,
**Rapporto mensile** e le viste per periodo. La riga sotto continua a dire su quanti chilometri
parla la media, e dove ogni viaggio porta la sua cifra — quasi sempre — il numero non cambia.
Niente viene ricalcolato.
