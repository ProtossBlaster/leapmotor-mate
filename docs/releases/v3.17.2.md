# v3.17.2 — a trip's summary in boxes, on every car

The layout [@michapr](https://github.com/michapr) built on the beta for the trip summary, from the
request [@gm27271](https://github.com/gm27271) opened in August (beta #31), now on every car — with the
price per litre back in it.

## What changes

- **The trip summary is laid out in boxes.** Distance and duration first, each in its own box. Then the
  electricity the trip used: the energy, the consumption and the price per kWh it was billed at. On a
  range extender the fuel sits beside it, in a box of its own: the litres, the L/100 km, what they cost
  and the **price per litre**. Then the total, with what the trip cost per 100 km. The figures are the
  same ones the card printed before, from the same sources — only where they sit changes.

- **The same card on an electric car, without the fuel.** On a car with no tank the electricity has the
  row to itself, with the energy recovered in braking. Its cost is printed once, in the total, because
  there it is the whole of it.

- **Sized for the narrowest column it gets.** On a computer the card is the first of three columns, and
  at a 1024 px window each box has about 73 px. Measured on the rendered page, not assumed: the price
  per litre has a line of its own, the kilometres the generator drove have a full-width line under the
  boxes, distance and duration step down a size only while the columns are that narrow, and no figure
  can break away from its unit, whatever the language.

## What does not change

No figure is computed differently. On a range extender the energy is still the cloud's getEC, never an
estimate from the battery percentage; the kilometres the generator drove still carry the line saying
they are a floor, counted from the fuel drop; and the electric rate of a generator trip still does not
appear on the card — getEC divided by the whole distance leaves out everything the generator fed to the
motor directly, and on real trips it reads 46, 82.7 and 386 kWh/100 km where the battery did not go
down at all. The electric-vs-generator bar of the beta layout is not included, for the same reason: its
electric share would be the distance minus a floor, a ceiling drawn as a fact.

The rest of the trip page is as it was — map, chart, trip details, consumption breakdown, notes.
Nothing stored changes, the database gains no column, and no MQTT entity is touched.

## Verification

- The full suite passes: **3 430 tests**, and CI is green on the commit of this change (`394f08f`).
- The new card has **20 tests**, each watched failing first. The guard that keeps the bar out was
  watched failing on @michapr's own file, which has it; the one that keeps two values of a row level
  was watched failing on a copy without the fix.
- Eight existing test files that read the old card were pointed at where each figure now sits; where the
  behaviour itself changed — the electric car's card, how two values of a row are kept level — the test
  says so. Five deliberate breakages of the template — the battery estimate on a range
  extender, the electric rate on the official build, a fuel figure allowed to wrap, the generator's
  distance without its floor line, a fuel box on an electric car — were each caught by the tests meant
  to catch them.
- Tried in a browser before it was written, with 123 real trips of a range extender and Mate's own
  demo electric car, side by side with the previous card, on a phone width and at 1024 px; then on the
  code itself in English, Italian and Polish. That is where the two defects fixed before release were
  found: distance and duration wrapped at 1024 px, and in Italian and Polish the cost per 100 km broke
  inside itself, leaving "km" on a line of its own.

No command was sent to any vehicle.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- Nothing to do after the update.
- Rolling back to v3.17.1 brings back the previous card. Nothing stored changes in either direction.

## Italiano

**Il riepilogo del viaggio a riquadri, su ogni auto** (beta #31, @michapr e @gm27271). Distanza e
durata, poi l'elettricità usata — energia, consumo e prezzo al kWh — e, su una versione con range
extender, accanto il carburante: litri, L/100 km, quanto sono costati e il **prezzo al litro**. Poi il
totale, col costo per 100 km. Sull'auto elettrica è la stessa scheda senza il carburante. I numeri sono
gli stessi di prima, dalle stesse fonti: cambia solo dove stanno. I km fatti col generatore tengono la
riga che dice che sono un minimo; il consumo elettrico dei viaggi col generatore resta fuori dalla
scheda, come prima; la barra elettrico/generatore non c'è.

La scheda è pensata per la colonna più stretta che le tocca — circa 73 px per riquadro con la finestra
a 1024 px — ed è stata provata nel browser in inglese, italiano e polacco. Tornando alla v3.17.1 torna la
scheda di prima; niente di quello che è salvato cambia.
