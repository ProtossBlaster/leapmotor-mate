# Mate 4.2.0 — cloud commands reach every model

What a car may do is now decided by the data its own cloud entry publishes and by the cloud's own
answer, never by the model name. The previous releases enabled cloud commands only where every
vehicle on the account was a B10, because only a B10 had been driven on-car. That was never a
property of the protocol: the command path of the updated Leapmotor cloud is the same one for the
whole range, and its reply says whether the command is permitted — a command a car has not got comes
back refused and the vehicle does not move.

## What changes

- **Commands work on every model.** The gate is the vehicle's own cloud data — declared abilities,
  account rights, control-module rights — plus the cloud's refusal. A refusal that says "no
  permission" is recorded for that car and that account, and Mate stops offering the control.
- **Sentry mode** is available where the account declares the right for it.
- **Switching the climate completely off** uses the payload each model was measured to obey: the
  bare value on the B10/C10, the same value inside the full seven-field body on the T03. Neither is
  reshaped, because each car ignores the other's form while the cloud reports success for both.
- **A car that under-declares its air conditioning keeps it.** The European T03 does not declare the
  air-conditioning ability and cools anyway, so that declaration is no longer used to hide the
  climate on any model.
- **A car measured without heated seats or heated steering keeps them hidden**, and the page, the
  stylesheet it injects and Home Assistant now use one rule instead of three.
- **Cloud per-trip history on every model**, including range-extender fuel per trip, collected with
  the session the installation already holds.

## What does not change

Acceptance by the cloud is not confirmation that the car carried a command out, on any model, and
Mate never retries a command whose outcome is unknown. On-car confirmation of physical execution
exists only for the B10. Existing credentials, PIN, migration decision, history and settings are
untouched; the vehicle's own declarations still decide what is offered, so a control your car does
not have does not appear.

## Verification

3973 tests in the environment the project pins, plus 210 in the independent cloud-client package.
Measured in the lab against the live cloud, on a real vehicle entry and reusing the session already
held: the contract's verdict is identical for B10, C10, C11, T03 and B05, command by command, and
the T03's full climate-off payload passes through unchanged.

## Update and rollback

Ordinary update: Docker and both Home Assistant add-ons use the same 4.2.0 payload, Desktop uses its
existing payload update. No credential, PIN or migration step is required. The database gains only
per-vehicle settings entries. Keep a pre-upgrade backup; restoring one discards data recorded since.

## Italiano

I comandi dal cloud arrivano a **ogni modello**. Cosa può fare un'auto lo decidono i dati che il
cloud pubblica per quel veicolo — abilities, diritti dell'account, modulo di controllo — e la
risposta del cloud stesso: un comando che quell'auto non ha viene rifiutato, e il veicolo non si
muove. Il rifiuto viene registrato per quell'auto e quell'account, e il comando non viene più
proposto.

La sentinella compare dove l'account ne dichiara il diritto. Lo spegnimento completo del clima usa
il payload che ogni modello è stato misurato obbedire: la forma nuda sulla B10/C10, lo stesso valore
dentro il corpo intero a sette campi sulla T03 — nessuna delle due viene rimodellata, perché ogni
auto ignora la forma dell'altra mentre il cloud risponde con successo a entrambe. Un'auto che non
dichiara il condizionatore ma raffredda comunque (la T03 europea) mantiene il clima; un'auto
misurata senza sedili o volante riscaldati li mantiene nascosti, e pagina, foglio di stile e Home
Assistant usano ora una regola sola. Lo storico dei singoli viaggi dal cloud, con il carburante del
prolungatore per viaggio, viene raccolto su ogni modello con la sessione già in corso.

L'accettazione del cloud **non** è la conferma che l'auto abbia eseguito, su nessun modello, e un
comando dall'esito sconosciuto non viene mai rimandato. La conferma in auto dell'esecuzione fisica
esiste **solo per la B10**. Aggiornamento ordinario, senza riconfigurare credenziali, PIN o
migrazione. Conservare il backup precedente.
