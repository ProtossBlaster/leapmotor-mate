# v3.17.3 — ✎ Manual is back where it was

A regression of v3.16.0, reported by [@termy91it](https://github.com/termy91it) on the add-on
([leapmotor-mate-addon #2](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate-addon/issues/2)): after the
update the price paid could no longer be typed by hand where it used to be, and every charge priced
that way came back asking to be confirmed.

## What changes

- **The charges priced by hand read ✎ Manual again, and are not asked for again.** A charge whose
  price you typed and that has no type is **✎ Manual**, as it was until v3.15.18 — on its badge, in the
  count of charges to confirm at the top of the Charges page, in the Home vs Public card and in the
  monthly report. Since v3.16.0 those charges read **❓ To confirm** and were counted as such, although
  their price was there all along.

- **The type menu has its ✎ Manual row again**, with the box for the total you paid, at the bottom of
  the menu where it used to be. Type the total, OK, and the charge is done in one gesture, as before.
  The ✎ beside the badge is the same box and stays.

- **The price stays separate from the type** — what v3.16.0 was for. Picking a type on a Manual charge
  keeps the price typed on it; typing a price on a charge that has a type keeps the type, and the cost
  carries the *billed* tag.

- **The Manual charges have a line of their own** in the Home vs Public card and in the monthly report:
  not public — a charge priced by hand may have been made at home — and not waiting either. In the
  monthly report they used to be counted as public. The search can filter them again.

## What does not change

Nothing stored changes, and there is nothing to do after the update: the charges priced before v3.16.0
read ✎ Manual again with the price they had, which was never lost — v3.16.0 kept every one of them.
Prices, types, energy figures, the AC vs DC card, the Statistics and Costs pages, the MQTT entities and
the export are as they were. The database gains no column.

## Verification

- The full suite passes: **3 460 tests**, the browser test included.
- **30 new tests**, each watched failing first on the previous code. One existing test file had pinned
  the regression — it asserted that a Manual charge counts as one to confirm — and was pointed at the
  behaviour restored. Five deliberate breakages of a copy of the code — the count at the top, the Home
  vs Public card, the monthly report, the answer to the price box, the guard that keeps an empty OK from
  repricing a charge — were each caught.
- Reproduced before it was fixed, on a database built with **v3.15.18** the way a Manual user built it,
  then opened by v3.17.2: *3 charges to confirm*, two of them priced long ago. The same database seen,
  with nothing clicked, by v3.15.18, by v3.17.2 and by this version: v3.15.18 and this version show the
  same badges, the same prices and the same *1 charge to confirm*; v3.17.2 showed three.
- Tried in a browser at 390, 1024 and 1280 px, in Italian, Polish and German: a price typed in the Manual
  row of a new charge turns it ✎ Manual; on a DC charge it keeps the type DC; an old Manual charge given
  its type keeps its price; an empty OK sends nothing. No errors on the page.

No command was sent to any vehicle.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- Nothing to do after the update.
- Rolling back to v3.17.2 shows the Manual charges as *To confirm* again; nothing stored changes in
  either direction.

## Italiano

**«✎ Manuale» torna al suo posto** (add-on #2, @termy91it). Con la v3.16.0 il prezzo pagato si scriveva
in una ✎ accanto al tipo, e nel farlo abbiamo tolto il modo in cui la gente lo usava: nel menu non c'era
più la riga *Manuale*, e tutte le ricariche già scritte a mano erano tornate **❓ Da confermare**, dentro
il conto in cima alla pagina. Era una regressione. Adesso una ricarica col prezzo scritto a mano e senza
tipo si legge di nuovo **✎ Manuale**, come fino alla v3.15.18, e non è da confermare: né sul suo
riquadro, né nel conto in cima, né nella card Casa vs Pubblica, né nel resoconto mensile. Nel menu del
tipo c'è di nuovo la riga **✎ Manuale** col campo del totale pagato. Dopo l'aggiornamento non c'è niente
da fare: le ricariche scritte prima della v3.16.0 tornano ✎ Manuale col loro prezzo, che non si era mai
perso. Il prezzo resta separato dal tipo: scegliendo un tipo il prezzo resta, e scrivendo un prezzo su
una ricarica che ha il suo tipo il tipo resta.
