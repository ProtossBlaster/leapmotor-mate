# v3.17.4 — the meter is read for as long as the charge is open

Reported by [@gm27271](https://github.com/gm27271) on a C10
([#295](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/295)): one home charge showed
**7.69 kWh from the wall against 10.03 kWh into the battery — a 130.4 % efficiency**, printed in
green on the Wallbox page. A charge cannot put into the battery more than the wall gave it.

The wallbox counter is in his house, on the same network as Mate. It never went away. Mate stopped
reading it.

## What changes

- **The counter is read because a charge is open, not because the car's cloud answered.** His
  charge opened at 20:35, the Leapmotor cloud failed at 20:38 and came back for nine minutes at
  22:04. The per-poll read sat behind the charging state, and a poll that fails never reaches it —
  so for 86 minutes nobody read a meter in the same room, and everything it did in the dark
  arrived as a single step at the end. A single step hides what a sequence shows: a counter that
  resets to zero looks exactly like one that rose, and the plausibility ceiling is far too wide to
  object to either. The meter is now read on every cycle the cloud could not serve.

- **A wallbox total measured through a blind spell is dropped, and the charge bills on its battery
  energy instead.** The minutes a charge spends open at the wallbox with no reading taken are
  counted; past ten minutes, the figure the counter ended on is not a measurement of that charge.
  Home Assistant unreachable while the car charges on, a poller restarted mid-charge while the
  cloud is dark, a container stopped for a few hours — all three used to pass in silence. This is
  the same answer a counter that ran away (#46) or stood still (#215) already gets, and it is
  decided on the clock and on our own reads, never by comparing the meter against the battery
  figure: that one rests on a battery capacity the owner types in, and the weaker number must not
  discredit the stronger one.

- **A charging efficiency above 100 % is no longer shown anywhere.** The charge card had hidden
  such a ratio since it was written; the Wallbox page printed the same impossible number and, its
  colour rule asking only whether the figure is at least 88, painted it green. One rule now serves
  the card, the session rows and the rolled-up totals. Both kWh figures stay on screen — comparing
  them is exactly how this was reported — and only the ratio is withheld.

- **The diagnostics bundle prints the unread minutes** as `dark=`, beside the two marks that were
  already there.

## What does not change

Nothing stored changes, and there is nothing to do after the update. Charges already recorded keep
their figures: they stop showing a ratio no page can claim, and are not recomputed. Prices, types,
the AC vs DC card, the Statistics and Costs pages, the MQTT entities and the export are as they
were. The database gains one column, used only to record time nobody measured.

## Verification

- The full suite passes: **3 480 tests**, the browser test included.
- **20 new tests**, each watched failing first on the previous code. Five deliberate breakages of a
  copy of the code — the read on a poll the cloud refused, the 100 % rule, the blind-minute
  threshold, the minute counter itself, and the guard that keeps a charge away from home off the
  house meter — were each caught.
- Tried in the page on a database carrying his figures: his charge reads *10.0 kWh in battery (DC)*
  with no percentage, and *7.69 AC · 10.03 DC · —* on the Wallbox page, while a healthy charge
  beside it still reads *efficiency 67 %*. Checked at 390, 1024 and 1280 px in Italian, Polish and
  German; no horizontal overflow.

No command was sent to any vehicle.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- Nothing to do after the update.
- Rolling back to v3.17.3 leaves the new column in place, unused, and shows an impossible
  efficiency again where one exists. Nothing stored changes in either direction.

## Italiano

**Il contatore della wallbox si legge finché la ricarica è aperta** (#295, @gm27271). Una ricarica
di casa mostrava 7,69 kWh dalla presa contro 10,03 kWh entrati in batteria: un rendimento del
130,4 %, impossibile, e per giunta scritto in verde. Il contatore è in casa sua e non è mai mancato:
era Mate a smettere di leggerlo. La lettura stava dietro allo stato «in carica», che un poll fallito
non raggiunge mai — così, caduto il cloud dell'auto alle 20:38 e tornato alle 22:04, per 86 minuti
nessuno ha guardato un contatore che stava nella stessa stanza, e tutto quel che aveva fatto è
arrivato in un colpo solo alla fine. Ora si legge a ogni giro, anche quando l'auto non risponde. In
più: se una ricarica resta aperta più di dieci minuti senza che il contatore venga letto, il suo
totale non è una misura e viene scartato, tenendo l'energia della batteria; e un rendimento sopra il
100 % non si mostra più da nessuna parte — i due valori in kWh restano a schermo, sparisce solo il
rapporto. Dopo l'aggiornamento non c'è niente da fare e niente viene ricalcolato.
