# v3.19.1 — a charge stays on screen, and a correction stops retyping it

Three defects, two of them reported by [@arzthilfe](https://github.com/arzthilfe) and the third
measured on a car of our own.

## What is fixed

### A charge in progress stays on screen when you turn the current down ([#307](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/307))

Turning a wallbox from 11 A down to 8 A in the middle of a charge emptied the Overview: only
*"cable connected"*, no remaining time, no power, until the session ended — and it came back on the
next charge, until the current was turned down again.

His log holds the exact moment:

```
14:48:13  State: charging | plug=1 chg=1 A=-3.4 | Frame age: 3s
14:50:15  State: charging | plug=1 chg=0 A=-1.6 | Frame age: 2s
```

**The frame is two seconds old**, so nothing was stale and the cloud was not repeating itself. What
flipped was Mate's own per-poll charging flag, because the charge-**detection** floor refuses a pack
current below it — the default is 2.0 A and at 8 A on the wallbox the pack draws 1.6.

That floor is there to notice a charge has **started**. Asked whether one already open is still
running, it answered no — and every charge block on the Overview reads that flag. It is the same
"one threshold, two jobs" as the 0.00 kW power reading fixed in v3.18.3, and this is its other half.

Everything underneath was right all along: the state machine stayed in CHARGING, the session stayed
open and the battery kept climbing while the screen said nothing. The status now reports charging
while a session is open **and the cable is still connected** — the cable is the guard, so a session
left open by any other defect cannot print "charging" for ever.

### Correcting a charge you typed in no longer retypes it ([#309](https://github.com/ProtossBlaster/leapmotor-mate/issues/309))

A charge entered by hand and marked **Home** came back **AC** the moment its owner corrected a cost,
a time or the SoC.

Both forms — the one that adds a charge and the panel that edits it — offered **AC and DC only**,
while the handler wrote the type from that same two-way choice. So Home, HPC and Free could not be
chosen on the way back in, and could not be created in the first place either.

The type is what the cost is computed from, what the statistics count by and what the Charges page
filters on. This was not a label going astray: it was a charge moving from one column of the
accounts to another because somebody fixed a typo.

Both forms now offer the five types Mate actually uses, and the type you pick is the one that is
kept. The AC/DC tag stays derived from it, because that one describes the socket and not the place:
Home and Free are AC, HPC and DC are DC.

### Kilometres driven while the poller was down are declared instead of dropped

Measured on a real B10: nine days with the poller stopped, in which the car drove **80 km** and was
also charged. On the first poll back Mate wrote a reconstructed charge for the battery — and nothing
at all for the distance. No trip, no offline gap; the odometer simply higher than before, and 80 km
in no page of the application.

A drive whose SoC went **up** cannot be rebuilt as a trip: the consumption would be impossible. But
the kilometres were really driven, and that is precisely what the offline-gap table exists to hold —
*measured, and attributable to no trip*. They are now recorded there.

The energy of such a gap is deliberately left empty: inside one SoC rise, how much was the charge
and how much the drive cannot be separated, and half a fraction is worse than none.

## Internal

Three things nothing was watching now have tests: the README's **version banner** — the sixth place
a release has to touch, which read v3.15.9 for eight releases without a single test going red until
[@jcconca](https://github.com/jcconca) noticed — the **parked-cadence slider**, which stopped at
300 s while the form accepted 600 and is raised to meet it (noticed by
[@arekm](https://github.com/arekm) and correctly left alone in his own pull request), and the bound
that tells a real position from the `(0, 0)` of a poll without a GPS fix.

## What does not change

- Nothing stored is recomputed or rewritten.
- No command is sent to any vehicle.
- A charge that was already reading correctly reads exactly as it did.

## Verification

- **3 660 tests pass**, thirty of them new, and every new test was watched fail first against the
  thing it guards — including the one that reads the rendered page, run against the old two-option
  form, and the banner test run against the v3.15.9 that had sat there for eight releases.

## Update and rollback

- Back up Mate using its existing backup feature before upgrading.
- Docker and Home Assistant use the normal image update while retaining the existing data volume.
- Nothing to do after the update.
- Rolling back to v3.19.0 brings the three defects back and changes nothing else; nothing stored
  differs in either direction.

## Italiano

**Una ricarica resta sullo schermo quando abbassi la corrente** (#307, @arzthilfe). Portare la
wallbox da 11 A a 8 A a metà ricarica svuotava la panoramica: restava solo *«cavo collegato»*, senza
tempo rimanente né potenza, fino alla fine della sessione. Il suo registro tiene il momento esatto, e
il fotogramma ha **due secondi**: non c'era niente di vecchio. A ribaltarsi era la decisione di Mate,
perché la soglia di **rilevamento** rifiuta una corrente del pacco più bassa — la predefinita è 2,0 A
e a 8 A il pacco ne assorbe 1,6. Quella soglia serve ad accorgersi che una ricarica è **iniziata**;
interrogata su una già aperta rispondeva di no, e tutti i riquadri della ricarica leggono quel
valore. È lo stesso difetto dei 0,00 kW corretti in v3.18.3 — questa ne è l'altra metà. Sotto, tutto
era giusto: la sessione è rimasta aperta e la batteria ha continuato a salire. Ora lo stato dice «in
carica» finché la sessione è aperta **e il cavo è inserito**: il cavo è la guardia.

**Correggere una ricarica scritta a mano non la ri-etichetta più** (#309, @arzthilfe). Una ricarica
segnata **Casa** tornava **AC** appena il suo proprietario correggeva un costo, un orario o il SoC.
Entrambi i moduli — quello che aggiunge e il pannello che modifica — offrivano solo **AC e DC**,
quindi Casa, HPC e Gratis non si potevano né scegliere né creare. Il tipo è ciò su cui si calcola il
costo, ciò per cui contano le statistiche e ciò su cui filtra la pagina Ricariche: non era
un'etichetta sbagliata, era una ricarica che cambiava colonna nei conti perché qualcuno aveva
corretto un errore di battitura. Ora entrambi i moduli offrono i cinque tipi veri, e il tipo scelto
è quello che resta. L'etichetta AC/DC continua a essere dedotta, perché descrive la presa e non il
luogo.

**I chilometri percorsi mentre il poller era fermo vengono dichiarati invece di sparire.** Misurato
su un'auto vera: nove giorni di fermo in cui ha percorso **80 km** e si è anche ricaricata. Al
ritorno Mate ha scritto una ricarica ricostruita per la batteria e **niente** per la distanza: né
viaggio né buco offline, solo un contachilometri più alto di prima. Un tratto il cui SoC è *salito*
non si può ricostruire come viaggio — il consumo sarebbe impossibile — ma quei chilometri sono stati
percorsi davvero, ed è esattamente ciò per cui esiste la tabella dei buchi offline: misurati, e di
nessun viaggio. L'energia di quel buco resta vuota di proposito: dentro una singola risalita del SoC
la ricarica e la guida non si separano, e mezza frazione è peggio di niente.

Niente di ciò che è salvato viene ricalcolato, e nessun comando viene inviato all'auto.
